Welcome to the Auction and Bidding System

Follow the steps below to prepare an auction, collect bids, close it, and record payments. The pages share the same auction, seller, item, bidder, and bid records.

Program Workflow

  1. Prepare an auction: Schedule it on Auctions, then register sellers and list their items. You can also add a seller's item from Auction Items.
  2. Browse items: Review or search the item catalog before bidding.
  3. Register bidders: Use Bidders to register a bidder and confirm payment of the UGX 30,000 participation fee.
  4. Start and bid: Start the auction from Auctions, then place or withdraw bids on the Bids page.
  5. Review and close: Check the highest bids and history, then close the auction on Winners & Payments. The system saves an item-by-item result.
  6. Settle payments: Complete pending winning-bid payments on Winners & Payments. The registration fee is separate and is not added to the winning bid a second time.
